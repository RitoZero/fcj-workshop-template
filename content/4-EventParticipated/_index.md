# Events Participated During Internship

During my internship, I participated in three remarkable events that expanded my technical knowledge, refined my professional mindset, and exposed me to cutting-edge industry practices. Each event delivered actionable insights and reinforced the importance of combining technical depth with soft skills.

---

## Overview of Attended Events

### [Event 1: Community Day 4/7 – Lessons Beyond Technology](4.1-Event1/)

**Event Name:** Community Day 4/7 – Lessons Beyond Technology  
**Date & Time:** 09:00 AM, July 4, 2026  
**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Saigon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City  
**Role:** Attendee  

**Core Value:** Extracted practical frameworks for navigating the tech job market, understanding SLA-driven monitoring, and leveraging AI as an accelerator while maintaining independent verification. Key takeaway: **visibility and proactive communication are as critical as technical expertise**.

---

### [Event 2: SLA, Monitoring, Automated Security with AWS](4.2-Event2/)

**Event Name:** SLA, Monitoring, Automated Security with AWS Security Agent & AWS Certification Pathways  
**Date & Time:** 09:00 AM, July 11, 2026  
**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Saigon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City  
**Role:** Attendee  

**Core Value:** Mastered the Monitoring Pyramid framework—from customer experience down to cloud infrastructure—and discovered how AI-driven security agents (AWS Security Agent powered by Amazon Bedrock) automate vulnerability detection across the full SDLC. **Key insight: healthy infrastructure ≠ happy users; end-user experience is the true north**.

---

### [Event 3: FCAJ – Agentic AI Build Week](4.3-Event3/)

**Event Name:** FCAJ – Agentic AI Build Week  
**Date & Time:** 09:00 AM, July 25, 2026  
**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Saigon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City  
**Role:** Attendee  

**Core Value:** Witnessed five high-impact hackathon teams (One team's F&B chatbot, Signal Scout's risk radar, Plan V's cloud architect AI, 3KA's crowd monitoring, and Team 5's e-payment flow) demonstrate that **AI is stepping out of the lab to solve real enterprise problems**. The hackathon spirit revealed that creative integration of APIs and cloud infrastructure, paired with team focus, breaks through technical barriers and delivers user value.

---

## Cumulative Outcomes & Lessons Learned

- **Technical:** Monitoring frameworks, AWS ecosystem (SLAs, automated security, cost estimation), agentic AI architecture, cloud integration patterns.
- **Soft Skills:** Workplace visibility, proactive communication, growth mindset, product ownership, rapid execution under pressure.
- **Mindset:** AI accelerates but never replaces verification; real product value lies in solving user pain points, not algorithmic complexity.
