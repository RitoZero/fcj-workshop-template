# Event Report: “Community Day 4/7 – Lessons Beyond Technology”

### Event Objectives

- Provide practical insights into the technology job market and essential preparations for young professionals entering the industry.
- Share cross-environment working experiences and system design thinking rooted in problem-solving.
- Guide attendees on how to build visibility and enhance communication skills in the workplace.
- Direct effective, non-dependent AI usage combined with a growth mindset and professional work attitude.

### List of Speakers

- Expert speakers and guest presenters from the technology community sharing insights on the job market, practical experience, and soft skills.

### Key Highlights

#### Practical Insights Into the Tech Job Market

- **Rising Recruitment Standards**: Even for internships, candidates need to clearly define their career goals, research active hiring sectors, and understand specific position requirements.
- **The Value of Networking**: Many job opportunities are shared internally or through referrals, making relationship building and self-visibility essential.
- **Cloud & AI Trends**: Enterprises continue migrating to the cloud with high talent demand, prioritizing experienced candidates. For students and juniors, AI serves as an accelerator to bridge gaps, provided foundational knowledge and result verification remain intact.

#### Lessons from Multi-Environment Work Journeys

- **Product Ownership**: Understand and take full responsibility for what you build.
- **Business Requirements First**: Grasping functional requirements often outweighs writing overly complex code.
- **Scale and Architecture**: Data scale can completely alter a system's underlying architecture.
- **Speed vs. Perfection**: Execution speed sometimes takes precedence over absolute perfection.
- **System Design Mindset**: Always start system design by clearly defining the problem before selecting tools.

#### The Art of Workplace Visibility

- **Redefining Visibility**: Visibility is not about drawing attention; it starts with simple actions like proactive greetings, asking questions, and actively participating in conversations.
- **Avoiding Silence**: Remaining silent prevents peers and managers from recognizing your capabilities and potential contributions.

#### Smart AI Usage & Growth Mindset

- **Independent AI Usage**: Understand why AI reaches a conclusion rather than blindly copying answers.
- **Growth Mindset**: Constantly ask questions, embrace mistakes as a natural part of learning, and continuously grow.
- **Employer Evaluation**: Companies look beyond technical skills to evaluate a candidate's attitude, experience, background, and growth potential.

### What Was Learned

#### Technical & Problem-Solving Mindset

- **Solid Foundations**: Technical knowledge is core but must be paired with proactive initiative and flexible problem-solving skills.
- **Tool Control**: Leverage AI and new technologies as learning multipliers while keeping logical reasoning and verification intact.

#### Soft Skills & Work Attitude

- **Proactive Connection**: Enhance visibility and interaction within team environments to assert personal value.
- **Lifelong Learning Attitude**: Technology constantly changes, but creating value, taking responsibility, and continuous self-improvement ensure better adaptability to market fluctuations.

### Application to Work

- **Proactive Learning & Foundation Enhancement**: Utilize AI as a research support tool while rigorously verifying all outcomes.
- **Enhanced Team Interaction**: Apply proactive communication, ask questions, and engage actively in technical discussions to boost professional visibility.
- **Growth Mindset Development**: Maintain an open attitude toward errors, viewing them as opportunities to accumulate experience and sustain long-term career growth in technology.

### Event Experience

#### Real-World Expert Perspectives
- Listened to practical workforce insights and valuable lessons drawn from various working environments.
- Gained a deeper understanding of corporate expectations for young talent amidst technological shifts.

#### Core Takeaways
- Technical expertise must go hand-in-hand with a positive attitude, accountability, and flexible adaptability.
- Relationship building and proactive communication are keys to unlocking career development opportunities.
